# Preserve existing arrangements during suspension

Suspension blocks a member from creating new posts or submitting new claims. The member may still log in, view history, and complete existing arrangements; suspension does not silently cancel outstanding reservations or invalidate their pickup tokens. Existing arrangements remain subject to their normal approval, cancellation, QR verification, and deadline rules.

We chose this behavior over cancelling all activity on suspension because other members depend on existing reservations. Admin post removal is a separate action for stopping pickups from unsafe or otherwise unsuitable posts. Reactivation lifts the suspension restriction.
