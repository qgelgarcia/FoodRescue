# Reserve portions when a claim is submitted

A pending claim immediately reserves its requested portions; donor approval makes it ready for pickup. We chose reservation at submission over allocation at acceptance so competing requests cannot promise the same portions, consistent with the documented immediate inventory decrement.

Donor rejection or donor-approved claimant cancellation releases its reservation. Released portions become available to new claims before the deadline unless the post has been cancelled or removed. When release makes portions available on a fully reserved post, the post returns to active and reappears in the feed. Each reservation is released at most once. For example, a pending request for all five sandwiches prevents a second claimant from requesting two until the first reservation is released. Detailed cancellation and expiration behavior is recorded in subsequent decisions.
