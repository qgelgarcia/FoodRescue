# Limit claims per person per post

A person may have at most one pending or accepted claim per post and may not claim again from that post after completing a pickup. A cancellation request awaiting donor approval does not remove the existing claim or its reservation, so it does not permit a second claim.

We chose this limit over repeated concurrent reservations or repeated pickups to preserve the agreed per-person boundary. Eligibility must be enforced in the shared claim operation rather than only in the interface. A person whose earlier claim was rejected or cancelled may submit a new claim while portions remain available before the deadline. The new claim requires donor approval, and earlier claims remain in history. A person who has completed a pickup remains ineligible to claim again from that post.
