# Require donor approval to cancel a pending or accepted claim

A claimant seeking to cancel either a pending or accepted claim must provide an explanation. The donor may approve or deny the request; the portions remain reserved while it awaits a decision. Approval cancels the claim, invalidates any pickup token, and releases portions for new claims before the deadline while the post remains available.

Denial leaves the claim in its existing pending or accepted state. For an accepted claim, its pickup QR remains valid, allowing collection before the strict deadline. At the deadline, pending claims are automatically rejected and accepted but uncollected claims expire regardless of a pending or denied cancellation request. We chose donor approval over unilateral withdrawal in both states to give the donor control over changes to food reservations.
